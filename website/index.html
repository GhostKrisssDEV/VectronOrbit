// Features

A living galaxy

Every sector has its own dangers, planets, bosses and rival pilots. Grind, trade, upgrade and push deeper into space.

🚀

8 PREDATOR WARSHIPS

From the agile Sparrow to the Titan dreadnought – each hull with its own ability and 13 two-colour liveries.

👾

ALIEN WARFLEETS

Drones, Stingers, Lancers, Wardens, Reapers and massive world bosses that drop blueprints.

🏴‍☠️

PIRATES & ACE PILOTS

Smart enemy pilots fire rockets, repair, retreat – and steal the cargo you drop when you die.

⛏️

MINE · REFINE · CRAFT

Mine six ores, sell them, refine them or overclock your lasers and shields. Build gear in the Assembly Lab.

🛰️

8 SECTORS

Home space, nebulae, ion storms and radiation – defeat the Hive Overlord to unlock wild space 2-1 to 2-4.

🤝

FREE CO-OP

Fly with 2-4 friends. Rewards are shared and every boss pays every pilot in full.
